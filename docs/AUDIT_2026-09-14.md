# CODERA — Live audit, 2026-09-14 (after Iterácia 4.2)

Diagnostic pass against the production build (`master a10a8dd`, served at
https://www.codera.sk and, for the automated probes, its deployment URL
`codera-ba902esqf-webora10.vercel.app` — same SHA; the www host challenges
repeated automation). Desktop 1440×900 and mobile 375×812 (touch, DPR 3),
headless Chromium with a GPU. Methods: console and network capture, axe-core
4 (wcag2a/aa + best-practice), per-frame text-state probe at every act's
reading moment (effective opacity, clipping, overlap, font size), luminance
per act, link resolution, resource timing, LCP/CLS via PerformanceObserver
(three runs on the homepage), scripted walks through the seams and the
enquiry drawer. Scratchpad tools `audit-42.js`, `audit-42b.js`; screenshots
in `audit-out/`. **Diagnosis only — nothing was changed in this pass.**

Legend: **CONFIRMED / PARTLY CONFIRMED / NOT CONFIRMED**, with the
implementation responsible.

## 1. Console errors and failed requests — NOT CONFIRMED (clean), with two warnings

- Zero console errors and zero failed or 4xx/5xx requests on all seven
  pages probed, both viewports. Every internal link resolves 200.
- Warning on every non-home page: the homepage's `<link rel="preload">`

## 4. Scroll — KEEP the response, CONFIRMED on length

- Input response: spring-followed passages, measured 2026-09-12 with real
  wheel input at DPR 2 — mean 16.9–17.3 ms, 0–2 jerks per seam. No synthetic
  scroll layer. KEEP. Trackpad (continuous delta) was not measured in this
  pass → VERIFY.
- **Length: 12 456 px of desktop scroll ≈ 125 wheel notches; the four seams
  are 5 400 px = 43 % of the journey with nothing to read.** Since 4.2 a seam
  is 150 svh (`app/city.css:194`); the acts themselves total ~7 000 px. On a
  trackpad the proportion is the same, the time longer. Whether it feels
  long is a DEVICE judgement; that almost half the page is passage is a fact.
  CONFIRMED.
- Walk end dead zone: NOT CONFIRMED — at timeline 1.0 the fifth card is at
  opacity 1 with `pointer-events: auto`.
- Phone: no traps found in scripted scrolling; the rail is a horizontal
  scroller with `scroll-snap-type: x mandatory` inside vertical scroll
  (`app/city.css:459`) — diagonal-swipe behaviour on iOS not testable here
  → VERIFY.

## 5. Interaction baseline — PARTLY CONFIRMED

- Mobile menu opens on screen (links at x = 20, 91 px tall) — the Step-4
  blocker is gone. KEEP.
- **The primary CTA ends in `mailto:`.** "Začať projekt" → drawer → the
  form composes a message and hands it to the visitor's mail client
  (`components/site/enquiry-form.tsx:80`, `deliver()`); nothing on the
  studio's side receives, stores or confirms the lead. The fallback copy is
  honest ("Ak sa neotvoril, napíšte nám priamo"). But a visitor on a work
  laptop with webmail gets an OS prompt or nothing, and the studio that sells
  "Funkčný formulár… ochrana proti spamu" in its Vizitka and Firemný web
  packages does not have one itself. Documented as a known state in the
  component header; still the largest conversion risk on the site. CONFIRMED.
- **Kancelária "ČÍTAŤ →" only exists on hover** (`group-hover:opacity-60`,
  `components/concepts/statut.tsx:350`) and the article is a `cursor-pointer`
  `<article>` with no link — nothing appears on touch and nothing happens on
  click anywhere. CONFIRMED.
- **Phone rail affordance**: five demos promised in the copy, one visible,
  the next card peeks ~2 px, no dots or arrows (`.city-railcard` basis
  `min(84vw, 560px)` + 1 rem gap, `app/city.css:465`). PARTLY CONFIRMED.
- **Tap targets under 44 px**: header nav links 34 px, "Otvoriť menu" 36×36,
  demo nav links 22–28 px, WordPress editor tabs 30 px, the KONCEPT label 14
  px. Responsible: `components/site/site-nav.tsx`, `wordpress-editor.tsx`,
  concept nav bars. CONFIRMED (minor, many).
- Booking, editor, cart, language, revisions: exercised by
  `tests/demos.spec.ts` and the PREVIEW scripts on 2026-09-12; working. KEEP.

## 7. Sections joined by effect — PARTLY CONFIRMED

The acts are authored as one line (show → offer → process → ask) and each
delivers a different kind of content (refokus). What joins them is only the
cloud passage, and at 43 % of the scroll the join is the longest thing on
the page (§4). The passages are now a flight, not a wipe (4.2) — the
question that remains is proportion, not kind.
