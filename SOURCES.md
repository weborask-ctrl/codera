# SOURCES — imagery and asset provenance

Step 6 phase E. One line per visual asset class, with its licence. The rule it
enforces: **CC0, purchased, or first-party only** — and as of 2026-08-31 the
answer everywhere is first-party, because the direction made photography
unnecessary: the concept worlds draw their imagery (packaging, documents,
timetables) as vector and typography, and the site's own visual material is
the ribbon, the fog and the type.
