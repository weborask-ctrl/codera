# Step 6 — Phase C: the three concept worlds

One decision-engine run per sector, then the build spec for each. Replaces
Konštrukt (construction), Vitalis (clinic) and Forma (interior studio), which
retire with their sectors.

Contract: `process/STEPS.md` Step 6 §5. Copy and staging: `CODERA_STEP6_CONTENT.md`.
Engine: `CODERA_DESIGN_INTELLIGENCE/DESIGN_DECISION_ENGINE.md`. Matrix rows:
`03_INDUSTRY_STYLE_MATRIX.md`.

---

## The rule this phase is judged by

All three are modern and staged in 5D — Ondrej, 2026-08-31. That is a shared
level of ambition, not a shared look. **The test: if the content of the three
worlds could be swapped and nobody noticed, the act has failed.** It would prove
one taste where it is supposed to prove range, which is the exact finding of
Step 4 §2.

So each world is pinned to three things it does not share with the others: a
**climate** (ground, light, temperature), a **type voice**, and a **density**.
Those three are listed first in every run below, and they are what the review
checks.

| | Meridián | Štatút | Vlna |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Climate | Warm roasted — bone, umber, ember | Cool institutional — stone, ink, oxblood | Bright studio — chalk, citrus, deep teal |
| Type voice | Editorial serif display, mono metadata | Institutional serif, tabular figures | Wide grotesque, tight and loud |
| Density | Generous, product-led, few large blocks | Dense, document-like, many small blocks | Airy, schedule-led, medium blocks |
| Mechanic | **Buy** | **Enquire** | **Book** |
| Motion at rest | Slow material drift | None — stillness is the point | Rhythmic, one pulse |

Names are placeholders until checked against the Slovak trade register and live
domains; all three ship on `*-koncept.sk` and carry the `Koncept` label.

## 1. Meridián — pražiareň kávy

### Engine run

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | Speciality coffee roastery with an online shop |
| Audience | Café owners buying wholesale; enthusiasts buying 250 g bags |
| Price positioning | Premium everyday — more than supermarket, far from luxury |
| Brand personality | Warm, precise, obsessive about origin |
| Product | A physical good with a short shelf life and a strong package |
| Trust requirement | Medium — freshness date, origin, roast profile do the work |
| Emotional target | Appetite, then confidence |
| Content | Many SKUs, short descriptions, origin data per bag |
| Assets | **No photography.** Packaging is drawn as vector — which is why this sector was chosen |
| Conversion | **Buy** — add to cart |
| Device | Mobile-heavy browsing, desktop for wholesale |

**Derived direction:** warm-editorial fused with ecommerce discipline;
retro-craft as the differentiator, held back from pastiche. Matrix row
*Wineries / craft food* applies more than *General D2C* — the buyer is choosing
a story, then checking the price.

### Direction

- **Climate.** Bone paper `#F4EFE6`, umber ink `#2A1D14`, one ember accent
  `#C4531F`. No white anywhere: the ground is always slightly toasted.
- **Type.** Editorial serif display for the origin name, mono for the origin
  data (altitude, process, roast). The contrast between the two *is* the brand.
- **Density.** Generous. Four products on screen, each large enough that the
  bag is the image.
- **Imagery grammar.** Drawn packaging: label geometry, origin latitude lines,
  a roast-level bar. No beans, no burlap, no steaming cups — every coffee site
  has those and none of them is this one.
- **Component language.** Product cards with the bag as a solid colour block
  and the label typeset directly on it; price and weight in mono, aligned right.
- **Motion.** Slow material drift only — the ground warms fractionally as the
  world settles. Nothing bounces.

### What the preview frame shows

Header with the wordmark and a cart count · one wide origin card (Etiópia,
Guji) with the drawn bag and the mono origin data · a three-up grid of smaller
bags below · a price and an *Do košíka* button in the ember accent. The frame
must read as a shop, not a landing page — the cart is the proof.

### 5D staging in /03

Ground turns to warm paper, light goes low and raking from the left so the
plane reads as physical stock. The plane holds a slight warm bloom at its edge.

## 2. Štatút — advokátska kancelária

### Engine run

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | Law practice, commercial and property |
| Audience | Company owners and managers with a problem that is already expensive |
| Price positioning | Professional, hourly, not published |
| Brand personality | Precise, unhurried, institutional |
| Product | Judgement and representation — nothing visual to show |
| Trust requirement | **Maximum.** The highest of the three |
| Emotional target | Relief that someone competent is in the room |
| Content | Practice areas, procedure explanations, team, publications |
| Assets | No photography, and none is wanted — stock lawyers in suits are a negative trust signal |
| Conversion | **Enquire** — a consultation request |
| Device | Desktop-weighted, often searched under stress |

**Derived direction:** corporate-institutional with heritage-editorial
differentiator, per the matrix row *Legal / law firm*. This is the world where
"modern" has to mean *contemporary institutional*, not *cinematic* — a law
practice that looks like a product launch loses the client at the first frame.
Modernity here comes from typography, grid discipline and restraint, which is
exactly how the current generation of institutional sites reads.

### Direction

- **Climate.** Cool stone `#EDEDEA`, near-black ink `#14161A`, one oxblood
  accent `#6E1F26` used at most twice per screen.
- **Type.** Institutional serif for headings, tabular figures for anything
  numeric — case references, dates, fees. Metadata in small caps.
- **Density.** The densest of the three. Multi-column, hairline rules, a real
  index of practice areas. Density *is* the competence signal here.
- **Imagery grammar.** Documents as objects: a numbered clause list, a
  hairline table, a seal-like mark. No photographs at all.
- **Component language.** Practice-area rows with numeric indices, a sidebar
  index, a quiet CTA that says *Nezáväzná konzultácia* rather than a pill.
- **Motion.** None at rest. Stillness is the design decision, and it is the
  strongest contrast to the other two worlds in the act.

### What the preview frame shows

A serif masthead with the practice name · a hairline nav of practice areas ·
a two-column layout: left, a numbered index of five practice areas with
tabular numbering; right, a short statement and the consultation CTA · a
footer strip with jurisdiction metadata. Legible at a glance as a document.

### 5D staging in /03

Ground turns to cool flat stone, light goes even and frontal — almost no
raking. The plane sits square to the camera. The act's only still moment.

## 3. Vlna — wellness a pohyb

### Engine run

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Industry | Wellness and movement studio — classes, memberships |
| Audience | Urban 25–45, deciding on a phone, often in the evening |
| Price positioning | Mid, subscription-shaped |
| Brand personality | Bright, energetic, welcoming — never boot-camp |
| Product | Time slots. The schedule *is* the product |
| Trust requirement | Low-medium — cancellation policy and a visible first-class price do most of it |
| Emotional target | "I could start on Thursday" |
| Content | Timetable, class types, trainers, membership tiers |
| Assets | No photography — colour, type and the grid carry it |
| Conversion | **Book** — reserve a slot |
| Device | Mobile-first, decisively |

**Derived direction:** high-colour-expressive with the soft-pastel pole for
warmth, per the matrix row *Sports / fitness* moved one step away from the
performance-lab dark cliché. The differentiator is that the schedule appears
immediately instead of being buried behind a "Rozvrh" link.

### Direction

- **Climate.** Chalk `#FBFAF7`, deep teal ink `#123B3A`, a citrus accent
  `#D8F24B` used confidently and often — the only world of the three where the
  accent is allowed to shout.
- **Type.** Wide grotesque, tight tracking, large. The width axis pushed
  further than Codera's own shell allows itself.
- **Density.** Airy, but the timetable is a real grid with real times.
- **Imagery grammar.** Colour fields and the timetable itself as the hero
  graphic. Motion-path arcs as decoration, never as content.
- **Component language.** Day tabs, slot chips with time and capacity, a
  three-tier membership row, a sticky booking bar on mobile.
- **Motion.** One rhythmic pulse on the live slot count. Nothing else.

### What the preview frame shows

Bold wordmark and a citrus *Rezervovať* button · a headline in wide grotesque ·
the timetable: day tabs across the top, four slot chips with time, class name
and free places · a membership strip beneath. The frame must make the next
available class visible without scrolling — that is the whole argument.

### 5D staging in /03

Ground turns to bright chalk, light goes high and open. The plane is the
brightest surface in the entire journey, which is what makes the act resolve
upward instead of trailing off.

## Build order

   the current ones are: `absolute inset-0`, `cqw` units, hard-coded palettes
   (they are *other* websites and must not follow Codera's tokens), zero image
   bytes.

   `public/work/*.jpg` used by the WebGL planes.
3. Rewire the five call sites: `components/site/scene-work.tsx`,
   `components/world/world-experience.tsx`, `components/world/ribbon-world.tsx`,
   `components/mobile/mobile-experience.tsx`, `app/textures/page.tsx`, plus the

   the one reasoning line, domain, `Koncept`.
5. Case-study pages are phase D and are not part of this build.

## Review checklist for this phase

- [ ] Swap test: the three worlds cannot be mistaken for one another
- [ ] Each preview reads as a *shop*, a *document* and a *schedule* respectively
- [ ] No photography, no placeholder grey blocks, no browser-chrome mockups
- [ ] Each palette is hard-coded and unaffected by Codera's own tokens
- [ ] `Koncept` label present in the stage, the metadata and the alt text
- [ ] Contrast: body and display text clear 4.5:1 on every one of the three grounds
- [ ] The previews still size correctly at both extremes — full-bleed on the
      work stage and ~160 px inside the hero phone frame
