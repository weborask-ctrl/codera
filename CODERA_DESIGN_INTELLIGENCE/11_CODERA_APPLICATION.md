# 11 — Codera Application

Applying the library to Codera itself (per the working document's
Step 1 §9): the outcome of the broad study turned into a direction for
Codera's own site — WITHOUT locking Codera into a full-dark aesthetic.

---

## 1. Diagnosis the library makes precise

The current Codera direction (spatial v2, "Ribbon Chamber") sits almost
entirely in the dark-cinematic family: graphite canvas, one warm accent,
a single 3D brand object, scroll-as-timeline. The library identifies the
exact cost of that choice:

- **Emotional monotone:** dark-cinematic's own record lists it — "a
  whole site in one dark register means no act lands as drama, and
  gloom accumulates." The catalogue's quality mass is 68% light; dark
  reads premium only AGAINST light.
- **Genre trap for an agency:** an agency site must showcase RANGE.
  A mono-dark shell implies one house style, which the portfolio
  section then confirms (all three concept projects currently read as
  darker/weaker Coderas — the same family, executed below the shell).
- **Density/readability tax:** long Slovak copy on graphite at small
  sizes is the hardest legibility mode we ship.

## 2. The prescribed direction: a dynamic contrast system

Codera's next direction is not "less dark" — it is a **scripted
dark/light dramaturgy** (05 §5, Apple/Wealthsimple pattern):

- **Dark graphite / black-titanium states** where cinematic depth earns
  its place: the opening act (brand object, spatial promise) and at most
  one mid-page immersion act.
- **Light and warm-light states as relief and clarity:** warm paper
  (#f5f1e4–#fdfcfc class) for the transformation story, offer, and
  conversion acts — facts, prices, and forms live in LIGHT. Dark→light
  transitions land as relief exactly where commercial clarity peaks.
- **Project-specific color:** each portfolio concept brings ITS OWN
  family and palette into its act (see §3) — the work re-colors the
  world, proving range in the mechanics of the site itself.
- **Controlled transitions as dramaturgy:** act breaks motivated by
  content (a reveal, a chapter turn), one mode owning ~60% of the page.
  The existing atmosphere-lerp machinery (graphite→paper→warm) is the
  right instrument — the correction is proportion and motivation, not
  technology.
- **A limited accent strategy:** ONE action color across the site;
  the warm metal of the brand mark stays a material, not a button
  color (05 §2, the BelArosa gold rule).

## 3. The portfolio is the range-proof

Each concept project gets designed AS ITS OWN CLIENT through the
DESIGN_DECISION_ENGINE, to Codera-level execution:

- a construction/industrial concept → industrial-architectural grammar,
- a health/wellness concept → warm-clinical grammar,
- a children's/consumer concept → playful/storybook grammar
  (or equivalents — the point is three DIFFERENT families, each with
  its own canvas, type strategy, and imagery language, all executed at
  the standard of the shell itself).

Codera's shell stays the stage; the projects are the colors. That is
the "demonstrate strong art direction while remaining capable of
showcasing very different client aesthetics" requirement, structurally.

## 4. What Codera keeps

- The parametric ribbon mark as the single hero object (brand ≠
  decoration, 08 §5) — one identity across 2D and 3D.
- Scroll-as-timeline architecture and the film/state machinery — with
  the scroll-economy audit applied (change per viewport, shorter
  pins, immediate input response).
- The typographic voice (Archivo-class wordmark discipline, tracked
  uppercase micro-labels) — extended with a warm-paper text ladder for
  the light acts.

## 5. What the library forbids Codera

- Full-dark monotony ("premium = permanent darkness" is refuted by the
  catalogue's own data).
- Motion covering weak static composition — every held state passes
  the paused-frame test, every text state gets a readable hold.
- Portfolio pieces sharing the shell's aesthetic by default.
- Synthetic smooth-scroll layers to mask input latency.
- Any invented trust data (registrations, clients, numbers).

## 6. Validation hooks

The Step 4 audit (current-site diagnosis) and later redesign steps must
check against this file: dark/light proportion scripted; light act
carries conversion; three portfolio families distinct; accent economy
singular; static-frame and text-hold tests passing; mobile re-directed
rather than shrunk.
