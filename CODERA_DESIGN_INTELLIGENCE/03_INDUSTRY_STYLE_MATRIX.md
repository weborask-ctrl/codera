## The two calibration examples (from the working document's gate)

**Construction company:** industrial-architectural chassis — pale
concrete or white canvas, grotesque type with tracked uppercase labels,
0–2px radii, honest frontal photography of sites and machines, corporate
trust layer (certifications, references, clear contact), ONE signal
color used like safety marking. NOT: dark-cinematic drama, playful
radii, thin grey text. The buyer is risk-averse and checks competence.

**Children's ecommerce:** playful-kids on a warm cream or white ground —
sticker radii (50px+), flat vivid accents mapped to categories,
character illustration in ONE style, big friendly product grids with
flavor mats, chunky CTAs, parent-facing trust strip (safety, returns)
in calm neutral. NOT: luxury austerity, dark mode, condensed display
faces, density. Two buyers at once: the child scans, the parent decides.

The matrix exists so these two briefs produce OPPOSITE designs from the
same library — that is the test of a working system.
