# Luxury / Fashion

**Archetype:** scarcity aesthetics — serif moments, ghost buttons, slow
rhythm, restraint as the price signal. Studied exemplars: Wealthsimple
("cashmere-lined vault"), BelArosa Chalet ("dusk on alpine vellum"),
AATHER, ddna ("warm museum vitrine on raw linen"), VISIONNAIRE, 21 TSI.

**Emotional tone:** poise, exclusivity, unhurried confidence, sensory
promise. The page behaves like a boutique: nothing begs.

**Typical audience / business fit:** premium hospitality, fashion,
jewelry, beauty, real-estate development, wealth management, craft
ateliers, any brand priced above its category and needing the design to
justify it.

**Poor-fit contexts:** discount positioning, utility software, urgent
conversion funnels, information-dense products, audiences who read
minimal chrome as "missing features". Luxury grammar on a cheap offer
reads as fraud.

**Canvas / background behavior:** warm parchment/linen (#ebe7e1, #efe3dc,
#f7f5ee) alternating with ONE deep atmospheric tone (mountain slate
#193741, wine, bronze #3a3525, forest). The dark tone is a mood, not a
theme — 1–2 full-bleed bands per page.

**Color logic:** colorfulness ≤5%. Two-color soul: one deep tone + one
warm metal/accent (honey gold, champagne, terracotta) that is DECORATIVE
ONLY — never a CTA fill (BelArosa: "never use Honey Gold as CTA fill").
Photography carries the remaining color. No semantic accent rainbow.

**Typography logic:** serif display + sans utility is the genre signature
(Giovanni+Avenir, Tiempos+The Future, Canela, GT Alpina). Serif ONLY at
display sizes — "a serif headline signals *this is a moment*". Sans
uppercase micro-labels tracked 0.125–0.167em. Display sizes moderate
(40–84px) — luxury whispers.

**Spacing / density:** the slowest rhythm in the taxonomy: 64–80px+
sections, one idea per screen, reading measures narrow, content sparse by
editorial choice. Density is the enemy of price.

**Layout / composition:** centered editorial stacks; 50/50 splits with
full-bleed photography; circular/arched image masks as jewelry; eyebrow →
serif headline → short body → ghost CTA as the repeating cell.

**Imagery direction:** atmosphere photography (golden hour, texture
close-ups, hands, materials); color-graded consistently; treated as
gallery objects with generous margins OR full-bleed mood walls. 3D only
as sculpture (Wealthsimple's draped forms). Never busy lifestyle collage.

**Component language:** GHOST/OUTLINED pill buttons as primary CTAs
(filled buttons read transactional); 80px+ pill radii or pure rectangles
— both work, mixing doesn't; hairline borders; no shadows ("depth from
color contrast and 1px borders"); forms minimal and concierge-toned.

**Motion behavior:** slow crossfades, soft parallax on photography,
letter-by-letter or line reveals used once (hero), hover states subtle
(fill-in of ghost button). Motion never bounces; it *settles*. Stillness
is a luxury feature.

**Mobile adaptation:** preserve the slow rhythm (do NOT compress section
gaps below ~48px); serif display clamps to ~32–40px; splits stack with
photography first; ghost buttons get full-width tap targets. Luxury
mobile fails when it becomes a dense app — keep the boutique pacing.

**Strengths:** justifies premium pricing; photography-forward; ages
slowly; low component complexity; strong differentiation from SaaS-look
competitors.

**Failure modes / common mistakes:** gold as button fill (instant
kitsch); two serifs fighting; dark band promoted to whole-site dark mode
(becomes gloom); stock photography breaking the spell; adding urgency
furniture (badges, timers) that murders the positioning; density creep.

**Combination guidance:** merges natively with warm-editorial (shared
canvas) and hospitality (shared imagery). Luxury × fintech/SaaS =
Wealthsimple pattern: keep the serif+cream, borrow SaaS component
discipline. Luxury × dark-cinematic: the dark band becomes a full act —
works for fashion (21 TSI) if text stays scarce. Never merge with
playful, pastel-friendly, or high-color families.
