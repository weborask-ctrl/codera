# Light / Editorial

**Archetype:** magazine logic on a white or near-white page — the type
scale IS the design. Studied exemplars: 14islands ("monochrome editorial
gallery"), AREA 17, Locomotive, Grafik, ACCOMPANY, jun.works.

**Emotional tone:** cultured, confident, curated, intellectual. A gallery
wall or a design annual. Quality is implied by what is *left out*.

**Typical audience / business fit:** agencies and studios, architecture,
publications, museums/culture, portfolios, fashion editorial, brand-led
B2B that sells taste. Audiences fluent in design; buyers hiring judgment.

**Poor-fit contexts:** feature-dense SaaS (nowhere to put density),
discount retail, audiences needing guidance and warmth (the austerity
reads cold), products needing constant CTAs — the genre resists loud
conversion furniture.

**Canvas / background behavior:** white / near-white (#ffffff, #f2f2f2)
or bone (#f0eeeb); full-bleed dark media blocks act as punctuation.
Inversion (ink↔paper) is the only "theme change".

**Color logic:** often literally two colors (Locomotive: #000 + #fff,
"0% colorfulness is a deliberate, ownable constraint"). A single mid-grey
for metadata (#a2a2a9 class). Photography supplies ALL chroma. If an
accent exists, it is one editorial ink (AREA 17's electric yellow) used
like a highlighter, never as UI state.

**Typography logic:** the 10× ratio — body 15–16px against display
75–180px, lh collapsing to 0.8–1.0, tracking to -0.04em. Single weight
(400) is common; hierarchy purely by size. Either one grotesque family or
grotesque + display face that never drops below ~30px. Uppercase tracked
eyebrows structure sections.

**Spacing / density:** extreme macro spacing (100–160px sections), tight
micro spacing (5–25px). Pairs, not grids — 2-column case-study layouts;
never 3+ columns. Max-width wide (1400px) or none (full-bleed type).

**Layout / composition:** full-bleed headlines that touch viewport edges;
asymmetric two-zone compositions (huge statement + small floating
paragraph); museum-catalogue image placement (centered image ~40% width,
labels floating in surrounding white); hairline rules as the only
dividers.

**Imagery direction:** commissioned-grade photography, full-bleed,
untreated, zero radius. Video blocks as movement punctuation. Every image
must look curated; one weak stock image destroys the genre.

**Component language:** text links over buttons (often NO filled buttons
in the whole system); 0–4px radii; hairline borders; tags as typographic
labels, not chips. Nav is a thin typographic bar.

**Motion behavior:** editorial motion = reveal and settle. Line-mask text
reveals, slow image unmasking, hover inversions. No bounce, no float.
Scroll should feel like turning pages, not riding a ribbon.

**Mobile adaptation:** display type steps down aggressively (clamp from
180px to ~44–64px) but keeps the ratio dominance; pairs become stacks;
full-bleed stays full-bleed. Text-link CTAs need enlarged tap targets.

**Strengths:** timeless; cheap to execute superbly (type + spacing, no
asset production); instantly signals taste; ages well; fastest path to
"this studio has judgment".

**Failure modes / common mistakes:** big type with body line-height
(oversized ≠ editorial); grid-of-cards creeping in; adding a "brand
color" that has no job; weak photography exposed by the austerity;
center-aligning long copy; letting CTAs disappear entirely on a
commercial page.

**Combination guidance:** the natural LIGHT ACT against dark-cinematic
heroes; merges cleanly with minimal-swiss (shares the grid) and image-led
(shares the gallery logic). With SaaS: editorial hero + product-voice
feature sections (Attio). Keep ONE canvas — editorial-on-white and
warm-editorial-on-cream must not alternate randomly in one page.
