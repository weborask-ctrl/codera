# Healthcare / Clinical

**Archetype:** calm, clean, trust through softness — the modern standard
is *warm-clinical*, not white-coat sterile. Studied exemplars: Headspace
("sunlit wellness retreat"), Letters ("morning clinic under open sky"),
2.AG ("clinical apothecary under teal light"), Finn (warm pet wellness),
A-WARE, August Health, Hims.

**Emotional tone:** safety, competence, care, low cortisol. The design
itself must lower anxiety — every choice is judged by "does this feel
safe AND kind?"

**Typical audience / business fit:** clinics, telehealth, pharma D2C,
wellness and mental health, medical devices (patient-facing), insurance
health lines, pet health, care services. Users often arrive worried;
trust requirements are maximal.

**Poor-fit contexts:** none within health — but the GRAMMAR fails when
exported to edgy/entertainment brands (reads bland) and when a serious
clinical context gets the playful end (a oncology clinic must not look
like Headspace).

**Canvas / background behavior:** two calibrated positions: (a)
warm-clinical — cream canvas (#f9f4f2) + white cards (wellness,
consumer); (b) cool-clinical — white + pale sky/teal washes (#d7e6f5,
frosted surfaces) for provider-grade seriousness. Full-dark is banned;
dark sections at most one per page.

**Color logic:** desaturated calm base + ONE trustworthy accent (surgical
blue, sage/teal, Headspace's single blue CTA rule: "Mindful Blue
exclusively for the single highest-priority action per view"). Warm
near-black text (#2d2c2b) in warm mode; navy-ish ink in cool mode.
Emotional accent (yellow, coral) allowed as bounded punctuation in
wellness, banned in acute-care contexts. Red only for true alerts.

**Typography logic:** humanist/geometric sans with round terminals
(Apercu, Sofia, Open Runde class); weights moderate; display 40–64px
with gentle negative tracking; NO condensed faces, no aggressive display
type; body generous (16px+/1.5+) — patients read carefully; serif only
in apothecary-editorial wellness (A-WARE).

**Spacing / density:** breathing room as a clinical instrument — 64px+
sections, roomy cards (32–48px padding); forms broken into small
digestible steps; density banned on patient-facing surfaces (provider
dashboards may be dense but structured).

**Layout / composition:** centered reassuring hero (short headline,
plain-language subtext, one CTA); card grids for services; step-by-step
"how it works" bands; trust strip (credentials, security) always present;
FAQ native to the genre.

**Imagery direction:** real, diverse, naturally-lit people; product/
treatment photographed like skincare (clean, warm); friendly filled
illustration for abstract concepts (Headspace's blobs); NO surgeon-with-
crossed-arms stock, no fear imagery, no body-shame framing.

**Component language:** soft radii (8–16px cards, pills for CTAs);
1px warm hairlines over shadows; big forgiving inputs with visible
labels, inline validation phrased kindly; checkmark-benefit lists;
badges for certifications. Error states never shout.

**Motion behavior:** minimal and soothing — soft fades, gentle expansion;
nothing sudden, nothing bouncing on serious surfaces; breathing-tempo
rhythm (Headspace literally animates at breath pace). Reduced-motion
parity is an ethical requirement here.

**Mobile adaptation:** majority of health traffic is mobile — one-hand
reachable CTAs, forms one-question-per-screen, tap targets generous,
performance on old devices (patients skew all-devices). Phone number
always one tap away.

**Strengths:** trust conversion; broad accessibility; warm-clinical
travels well across health verticals; calm design measurably reduces
form abandonment.

**Failure modes / common mistakes:** sterile white-coat coldness (2010s
hospital sites); OR over-warming acute contexts into lifestyle fluff;
jargon in headlines; density panic (cramming every service above the
fold); dark-mode "premium" experiments (gloom + health = dread); tiny
grey legal text hiding critical info.

**Combination guidance:** warm-editorial is the native partner
(→ Headspace); minimal-swiss lends provider-grade rigor (→ Letters,
shadcn-class clinical blueprints); organic-natural for holistic/plant
positioning; pastel-friendly for pediatrics. Never dark-cinematic,
never brutalist, never high-color-expressive at full volume.
