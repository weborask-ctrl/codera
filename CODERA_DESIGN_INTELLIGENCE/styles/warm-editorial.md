# Warm Editorial

**Archetype:** the cream-paper school — warm off-white canvas, warm ink,
sunlit calm. The largest quality territory in the catalogue (525 matches).
Studied exemplars: ElevenLabs ("Bauhaus studio notebook"), Evernote
("afternoon sun on cream paper"), Intercom, Coda, Steep, Seline,
Wealthsimple's canvas, MindMarket's ground.

**Emotional tone:** composed warmth, literate confidence, human but
precise. A well-lit studio desk; a quarterly journal. Premium *without*
darkness — the direct counter-argument to "premium = black".

**Typical audience / business fit:** modern SaaS wanting warmth, AI
products softening their edge, knowledge/productivity tools, editorial
brands, consumer services with taste, fintech that wants trust+warmth.
Extremely versatile — the current default of high-end product design.

**Poor-fit contexts:** hard-industrial subjects, discount/urgency retail,
nightlife/entertainment, contexts demanding maximum data density (the
warmth needs breathing room to register).

**Canvas / background behavior:** NEVER pure white — eggshell/cream
(#fdfcfc, #f9f6f2, #faf8f5, #f5f1e4). White becomes the ELEVATED surface
(cards, nav) — a free elevation level with zero shadows. Optional deeper
step (#ebe8e4 class) for recessed bands; one dark or saturated band per
page as contrast punctuation.

**Color logic:** warm neutrals ladder + warm near-black ink (#2c2e2a,
#32302f — never #000000 for body); ONE accent with energy (Evernote's
lime, Intercom's blue, Coda's orange) at one-element-per-viewport
discipline. Decorative accents (illustration, footer band) stay separate
from the action color.

**Typography logic:** two proven voices: (a) whisper-weight display —
300–400 weight at 48–72px, tight tracking (ElevenLabs, Evernote: "weight
300 is the signature, heavier feels corporate"); (b) serif display over
sans body (Steep, Wealthsimple). Body 15–16px/1.5. Opposite-direction
tracking (display negative, body slightly positive) is a recurring
refinement.

**Spacing / density:** generous — 80–125px sections, 32px card padding,
1200–1280px max-width. Comfortable reading measure; density lives inside
white cards, never on the raw cream.

**Layout / composition:** centered hero stacks or asymmetric
headline-left/body-right; alternating cream/taupe bands instead of
borders; card carousels; hairline-ruled 3-column feature rows (rule on
top, no card chrome).

**Imagery direction:** product UI in framed cards; soft-gradient product
orbs; restrained illustration. Photography, when present, is warm-lit and
domestic. Grayscale logo strips. Never harsh studio-black imagery.

**Component language:** pill buttons (9999px) or soft 5–10px; dark filled
CTA on cream flipping to light/accent on dark bands; hairline #ebe8e4
borders; 16–24px card radii. Sticker-shadow (2px solid offset) as a
playful printed depth cue where friendliness is wanted.

**Motion behavior:** gentle and settling — fades, soft rises, no
aggression. The genre's stillness is part of its calm; motion budget is
small and spent on hero and state changes.

**Mobile adaptation:** survives shrinking better than most (few heavy
assets); keep cream canvas and white-card elevation; collapse carousels
to swipe decks; maintain the warm ink (small cold-grey text on cream
breaks first).

**Strengths:** premium without gloom; superb long-form readability;
flexible from SaaS to editorial; warm canvas + white cards solves
elevation for free; ages slowly.

**Failure modes / common mistakes:** pure-white sections leaking in and
killing the paper feel; cold greys on warm canvas; accent promoted to
backgrounds; whisper-weight display bolded "for emphasis"; density dumped
on raw canvas instead of cards; beige monotony with no contrast band —
the page needs one dark or saturated act.

**Combination guidance:** the ideal LIGHT/RELIEF ACT for dark-cinematic
openings (dark hero → cream body is the Apple/Wealthsimple dramaturgy);
merges natively with healthcare-clinical (→ warm-clinical), luxury
(→ boutique), playful (→ storybook). With brutalist type it produces the
fashionable "warm brutalism". Keep ONE ink temperature across the merge.
