## Why it works

Raw graphite tech-noir with total conviction: one dark world, type set small
and engineered, the WebGL surface behaves like an instrument, not a showreel.
Credibility comes from restraint — the studio that built half the genre shows
almost nothing and lets density of finish speak.

## What we take / what we refuse

TAKE: conviction of a single world; engineered smallness of UI type against a
vast canvas; the sense that the canvas is an instrument.
REFUSE: the near-total absence of commercial legibility — Codera sells to SMB
owners who need the offer readable in one pass.
