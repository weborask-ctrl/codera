## Why it works

"Blueprint on a white drafting table": an interactive property plan
rendered as a clean bichromatic system — one ink, one pen, one highlighter.
Discipline, not decoration, makes it read as a plan.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: the one-ink / one-signal register for the wall-section act; the idea
of the plan as an interactive surface (the floor-plan minimap). Refuse: the
bold 700 display (EcoDomček whispers), the pill dialect, and pure white
(the paper is warm).
