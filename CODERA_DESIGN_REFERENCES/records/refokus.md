## Why it works

Near-black editorial with two voices: engineered grotesque for structure,
a warm serif for human moments (testimonials, founder stories). Colour lives
ONLY inside project cards — the shell never competes with the work. WebGL
appears as accents woven between DOM sections, not as one monolithic canvas.
The site feels rich because every scroll beat delivers a different kind of
content, not a different effect.

## What we take / what we refuse

TAKE: the two-voice type system (grotesque structure + serif humanity); colour
strictly inside the work; WebGL as woven accents; beat variety over effect
variety. REFUSE: the sheer page length — Codera's budget is ~8 viewports.
