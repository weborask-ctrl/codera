## Why it works

"An architect's sheet that learned to glow blue": matte white, pure black
ink, one bolt of colour; dark sections act as showcases, a single sphere is
the only ornament. Hierarchy from font selection and weight, not size.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: the single-signal-colour discipline for technical annotation; semi-mono
as UI chrome; dark band as a "showcase, not a page". Refuse: the oversized
radii, the display-face spectacles, and the blue (EcoDomček's signal is
moss green on warm paper).
