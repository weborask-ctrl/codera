## Why it works

The 3D is the PRODUCT DEMO, not decoration: playful physical objects with real
materials (gloss, rubber, weight) respond to the pointer inside a dark stage
block, while the chrome around the stage stays calm lavender-white. The page is
a gallery wall with one live exhibit per frame. Aeonik carries everything in
two sizes; nothing shouts, so the exhibit does. Latency is imperceptible —
input maps to motion instantly, cinematics happen around that mapping.

## What we take / what we refuse

TAKE: the stage-block grammar (a dark 3D stage set INTO a calm chrome, not a
dark page); pointer-reactive physical materials; two-size type discipline.
REFUSE: the playful toy-like object language — wrong temperament for Codera's
"presné remeslo" positioning.
