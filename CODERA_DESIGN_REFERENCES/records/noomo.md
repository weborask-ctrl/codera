## Why it works

Glass 3D objects pass between the letters of an enormous grotesque — the
3D serves the typography, not the other way round. Light, airy, elegant;
the closest of the 5D references to Codera's own voice.

## What we take / what we refuse

Take: the object woven through the headline, 3D as a tangible material of
the layout. Refuse: the agency-portfolio framing — our demo worlds are
concrete fictional venues, not a studio showreel.
