## Measured (from the refero DESIGN.md record, read in full)

- Ground: #bababa warm mid-grey canvas; #ffffff inset paper; #1e1e1e lacquer
- Type: Matter · weight 400 only, every size; Matter Mono 10–12px with
  0.02–0.04em tracking and tnum for labels, nav, buttons, product codes
- Zero chromatic accent — "colour is the photography's job"
- Buttons: 1px outlined rectangles, 3px radius; everything else 0px
- One photograph per 60–100 % of the viewport; 100–180px section padding

## Why it works

"The UI is the mount, never the artwork." A material library whose chrome
disappears so stone and pigment dominate; mono labels give it a spec-sheet,
architectural-drawing quality. Image-then-text, never the reverse; a slow
museum walk.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: mono metadata voice for material names and specs (drevovláknitá
izolácia, boritá soľ, Rhombus, Fundermax); image-then-text order; single
weight display; sharp edges. Refuse: the grey canvas (EcoDomček's paper is
warm) and the total absence of an action colour (one green button is
required).
