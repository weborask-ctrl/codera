## Why it works

"Museum-quality darkroom gallery meets technical blueprint": a full-bleed
photographic hero with whisper-weight giant headlines, a black band where a
wireframe illustration floats, then a calm light product surface. The
dark → light rhythm is content-motivated and the type never shouts.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: weight-400 display at large sizes; the dark → light act rhythm
(the dusk close then the paper contact panel); wireframe/blueprint
illustration as the technical register. Refuse: the pure black void and the
four categorical accents — EcoDomček has one needle.
