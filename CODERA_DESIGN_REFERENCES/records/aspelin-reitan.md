## Why it works

A portfolio of built places pinned to a cream wall: every major section is
either a full-viewport photograph with one line of type bottom-left or a
parchment block with contained content. The building carries the brand;
the UI is hairlines and captions. Dark photographic breaks alternate with
cream content, so the page has a gallery-walk rhythm without a dark theme.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: one line of type over a full-bleed subject (the model); the plate
treatment for real photographs; parchment/walnut as the light/dark pair;
the gallery-walk rhythm. Refuse: the 40px display ceiling (the hero needs
one larger statement) and the no-filled-CTA rule (a construction enquiry
needs one loud green button).
