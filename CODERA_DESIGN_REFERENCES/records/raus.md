## Why it works

A cabin brand that reads as a hand-printed field journal: cream paper,
whisper-weight display, photography beside text rather than under it, the
category label as the headline ("IDEA 32"). The green is rare enough to
count. Depth comes from cream-on-cream layering, not elevation.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: warm paper as the world's ground; weight-300 display with tight
negative tracking; green used rarely so it counts; meta labels before copy.
Refuse: the 20–40px radii (planks, not pebbles — EcoDomček stays sharp) and
the booking-bar grammar (no booking flow here).
