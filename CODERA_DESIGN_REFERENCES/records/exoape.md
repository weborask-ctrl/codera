## Why it works

The poetic pole of the genre: a dusk world where full-bleed imagery, a LIGHT
display weight (Lausanne 300 at huge sizes — confidence through lightness, not
boldness) and enormous negative space read as cinema. Transitions are slow
dissolves, never wipes. The dark is warm and photographic, not flat #000.
Typography floats OVER the world and stays fully readable — light weight, big
size, generous leading.

## What we take / what we refuse

TAKE: light-weight display type at massive scale (the exact opposite of v2's
semibold-everywhere — this is the typography answer); dusk warmth instead of
flat black; text floating over the world at full opacity; slow-dissolve
transitions. REFUSE: photography dependence — Codera has no photo assets; our
imagery is the drawn worlds and the ribbon.
