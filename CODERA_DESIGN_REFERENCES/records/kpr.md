## Why it works

Huge white grotesque woven BEFORE and BEHIND the central figure — the
type and the world occupy the same space, so the page reads as one scene
instead of text over a picture. Film-like chapters give the scroll a
dramaturgy.

## What we take / what we refuse

Take: the type-layering (one headline line reads as sitting behind the
central object), the chaptered scroll. Refuse: the illustrated character
as the centrepiece — Ondrej's constraint is 5D, so the central motif must
be a real, living WebGL object (the Saturn of Ukážka Animácie & 3D).
