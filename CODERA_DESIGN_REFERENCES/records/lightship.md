## Why it works

"Cinematic open-road diorama": a product (a trailer) shown in landscapes on
warm cream, thin type whispering across the frames, one ember accent like a
campfire. Cinematic without darkness — a light canvas carrying a physical
product in its environment.

## What we take / what we refuse (EcoDomček candidate)

Take: the light-canvas cinematic register; the split headline for the hero;
product-in-environment as the first frame (the house in its garden).
Refuse: the bold 700 display, the 20px card radii, and the offset-collage
grids (EcoDomček uses plates in rows).
