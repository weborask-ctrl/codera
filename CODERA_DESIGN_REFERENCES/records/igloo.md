## Why it works

The strongest single idea in the set, and NOT dark: a luminous monochrome
FROST world. One environment (snow, mist, ice), one material story, the object
glowing from within, and mono type used as technical annotation — measurement
lines, coordinates, // comments — laid over the 3D like an engineer's drawing.
The atmosphere carries emotion while the annotations carry precision: exactly
the cinematic-yet-credible pairing Codera claims. Every beat stays in the same
world; "acts" are camera positions and light changes, nothing else.

## What we take / what we refuse

TAKE: light-as-protagonist in a monochrome atmosphere; ONE environment where
acts are camera + light changes (the coherence answer); mono annotations as
the signature texture — it matches Codera's existing coordinates/labels and
"presné remeslo"; fog as depth cue. REFUSE: the sound layer and the
crypto-brand tonality.
